Resize a symbol by dragging any corner of its selection box, continuously from 25% to 400%, with wires, terminals and the label following live; one drag is one undo step, and the size catches on 100% on the way past. The properties panel's Size is now a typed percentage. New store API: `previewPlacement` / `endGesture` for transient, single-undo drags.
