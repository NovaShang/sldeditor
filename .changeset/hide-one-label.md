Hide one element's label. `Element.labelHidden: true` drops that element's whole label block (name/ID and params) on the canvas and in the SVG/PNG/PDF and DXF exports, independent of the document-wide `meta.labelMode`. The property panel gains a Label row (Shown / Hidden). Previously the only per-element lever was clearing the name, which brings the ID back.
